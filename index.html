About Me

[PROFILE]

I’m a Motion Designer and Compositor with experience across 2D motion design, 3D product visualisation and VFX compositing.

I enjoy taking ideas from a rough concept to a polished final piece, whether that means building a product in 3D, bringing typography to life, or making a shot feel like it belongs in the real world.

I’m comfortable working independently or as part of a team, and I’m used to adapting to different creative workflows and tight deadlines. My background in compositing also means I tend to obsess over the little details that make a piece feel finished.
